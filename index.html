THE MISSING NARRATIVE

A four-stage ePCR documentation escape challenge · 2-person teams

Billing hold: 4 charts flagged

Every report contains documentation gaps. The eight omission choices and four evidence cards are shuffled each time you enter a station. Expect 30–45 minutes of active investigation and chart repair. Your team must find the critical omissions, explain the billing and continuity-of-care consequences, and describe a safe correction plan. Never invent findings, times or treatments.

Your answers stay on this device. This is a self-contained training game, not an ImageTrend connection or billing system.
